    FAQ

    Your brain
    wants answers.

    What kinds of apps do you work with?

    Games and utilities on Google Play and the App Store: kids’ learning, racing and action games, remote and mirroring tools, AI and productivity apps. If your app lives in a store, it can be optimized.

    How long until I see results?

    It depends on the niche and starting point. In my case studies, movement showed within weeks and compounded over 1–4 months. Larger jumps usually come once country-level and localized listings roll out.

    Is ranking guaranteed?

    No honest ASO expert can guarantee a position, because stores control the algorithm. What I guarantee is a proven process, transparent reporting and decisions backed by data.

    Do you provide reports?

    Yes. You get clear keyword-rank, visibility, download and conversion updates so you always know what changed and why.

    Can you handle paid marketing too?

    Yes. I run Google UAC campaigns, but always ASO-first: a listing that converts organically makes every paid dollar work harder.

    What do you need to get started?

    Your app link, Play Console / App Store Connect access (view-only is often enough for an audit), and your growth goals. I’ll send a free audit first.
